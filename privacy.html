Sonos Chat

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Sonos Chat does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it sends nothing beyond your home network.

Voice messages

The app uses your iPhone's microphone only while you are holding the record button. Each recording is saved temporarily on your iPhone, then played to the speakers you selected over your local Wi-Fi network. Recordings are never uploaded to the internet or sent to us or anyone else, and each one is overwritten by later messages.

Your speakers and local network

The app uses Local Network access to find and control the Sonos speakers on your Wi-Fi network. To show your speakers quickly next time, it remembers their names and network addresses on your iPhone. This information stays on your device.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the chime, appearance and per-room volumes, are stored only on your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. We don't operate any servers for this app and receive no information about you or how you use it.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
