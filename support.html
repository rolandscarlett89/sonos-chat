Sonos Chat

Support

Need help? The answers to the most common questions are below. If they don't solve your problem, email roland.scarlett@gmail.com and say which iPhone and Sonos speakers you're using.

Getting started

  1. Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi network as your Sonos speakers.
  2. Open Sonos Chat and allow Local Network access when asked. Your speakers appear automatically.
  3. Tick the rooms you want to reach, or choose Everywhere.
  4. Hold the microphone button, speak, and let go. Allow microphone access the first time.

Common questions

No speakers are found
  • Check your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the speakers. Guest networks often keep devices apart, so they won't work.
  • Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure Sonos Chat is turned on.
  • Turn off any VPN, then tap the refresh button in the app.
  • Make sure the speakers work in the Sonos app.
"No sound detected — message not sent"

If the recording is silent, the app doesn't send it, so an accidental press won't play through the house. Hold the button and speak clearly towards your iPhone.

"Too short — hold the button and speak"

Very short taps are ignored. Keep holding the button for the whole time you're talking, and let go when you've finished.

How do I cancel a message?

While you're recording, slide your finger off the button. The message is thrown away and nothing is sent.

My music didn't come back after a message

Most sources, including radio, streaming stations and your queue, resume automatically. AirPlay can only resume if the device that was sending it still has its session open. If not, the app shows "couldn't resume previous audio" and you can restart playback from that device.

The message is too loud or too quiet

Each room has its own message volume slider. It only affects intercom messages: your speakers go back to their previous volume afterwards.

Can I try the app without Sonos speakers?

Yes. Tap Try Demo Mode on the "No Speakers Found" screen, or turn on Demo mode in Settings. Messages then play on your iPhone instead of on speakers.

Privacy

Sonos Chat collects no data. Messages travel only from your iPhone to your speakers over your home network. Read the privacy policy.
