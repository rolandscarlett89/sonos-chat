Sonos Chat

A push-to-talk intercom for your Sonos speakers. Hold the button, speak, and your voice plays in the rooms you choose. Then the music carries on.

  • Any room, or every room. Pick rooms one by one, or call everywhere at once.
  • Your music waits. Playback pauses for the message and then resumes.
  • Instant. Recording starts as soon as you press. Slide off the button to cancel.
  • Private. No accounts and no tracking. Messages never leave your home network.
